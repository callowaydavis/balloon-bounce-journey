How to play

Tap just below the balloon to bump it up. Tap its top to push it down, and its sides to steer. Float out of one side of a room to reach the next.

Look out for cacti, candles, needles, fans, open windows, doors, a cat and a drone. In Journey mode, survive each level until its door opens, and make it through the front door to win. In Endless mode, see how many rooms you can pass.

Need help or found a bug?

Please open an issue and tell us what happened, which iPhone or iPad you have, and which mode and difficulty you were playing. A free GitHub account is needed to post.

Common questions

How do I turn off sound or vibration? Use the Sound and Haptics buttons on the title screen or in the pause menu.

Are there ads or purchases? No. The game is free, with no ads and nothing to buy.

Where do the leaderboards come from? Endless mode uses Apple's Game Center, with one leaderboard for each difficulty. Sign in to Game Center in your device's Settings to appear on them.

Is my progress saved? Your settings and best scores are saved on your device. If you delete the app, they go with it.
