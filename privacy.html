Privacy policy

Balloon Bounce Journey

The short version

Balloon Bounce Journey does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

On your device

Your settings (sound, haptics, difficulty and mode) and your best scores are saved on your device only. They never leave it, and they are removed if you delete the app.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your Endless-mode scores are sent to Apple's Game Center service so they can appear on the leaderboards. That is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The game does not receive your Game Center account details beyond what Game Center shows on a leaderboard.

No ads, analytics or tracking

The game has no ads, no analytics and no third-party code, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the change reaches the app.

Please open an issue.
